What is this?

Traveler's Rest is a small, independently-run fan space for people who play Genshin Impact and/or Wuthering Waves. We share builds, farming routes, event reminders, and mostly just hang out. No spoiler bait, no drama — just a place to talk about the games with other fans.

The games

Genshin Impact

Open-world gacha RPG by HoYoverse. Teyvat, elemental reactions, and a lot of banners.

Wuthering Waves

Action gacha RPG by Kuro Games. Fast combat, Solaris-3, and Echoes to collect.
