Guidelines

Community guidelines

Short version: be decent to people.

  • No harassment, hate speech, or targeted negativity toward players, creators, or the dev teams.
  • Spoiler-tag major story content for a reasonable window after release.
  • No leaks of unreleased material that developers have asked not to be shared.
  • No spam, self-promo without asking, or unsolicited DMs.
  • Keep discussion of monetization/gacha rates civil — frustration is fine, pile-ons aren't.

Moderators can use their judgment beyond this list. If something feels off, flag it to a mod rather than letting it escalate.
