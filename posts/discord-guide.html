Getting the most out of the Discord

Once you join the Discord, here's a quick orientation:

  • Pick your game roles so people know whether you're here for Genshin, Wuthering Waves, or both.
  • Check the pinned messages in each channel before asking a question — it's probably answered there.
  • Builds and team comps have their own channels, separate from general chat.
  • Event and banner reminders get posted as they're announced.

This page will get updated as the server structure changes, so check back if something here looks out of date.
