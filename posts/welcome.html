Welcome to Traveler's Rest

Hey, and welcome! This is a small, unofficial fan blog and Discord community for people who play Genshin Impact and/or Wuthering Waves. It's run by fans, for fans — there's no official affiliation with HoYoverse or Kuro Games.

The goal here is simple: a low-drama place to talk about builds, farming routes, upcoming banners and events, and whatever else comes up. The blog will get the occasional post, but most of the activity happens in the Discord.
